INSTALLATION COMPLETE — WHAT REMAINS?
======================================

What Remains? has been successfully installed.

GETTING STARTED
- Launch the game from your Desktop shortcut or Start Menu
- Create an account to save your progress and appear on the
  global leaderboard
- Use WASD or Arrow Keys to move
- Press E to interact with objects
- Press ESC to pause

THE STORY
You are a citizen of a dying city making impossible decisions.
Until you realise you are not a citizen at all.

There are three endings. One of them requires you to do nothing
for two minutes. You'll know when.

CONTROLS
  WASD / Arrow Keys — Move
  E                 — Interact
  ESC               — Pause
  1 / 2 / 3         — Make a choice

SAVE DATA
Your progress is saved automatically. Your save file is stored
locally on your computer and will persist between sessions.
